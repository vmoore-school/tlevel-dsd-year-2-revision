# Mock Brief 03: Northgate Community Food Bank (short practice brief)

> Original practice scenario.

**Northgate Food Bank** is run by volunteers. Stock is tracked on paper, so volunteers don't know what
is running low and donors don't know what to give. Referral agencies email vouchers that are checked
by hand.

The coordinator wants a solution that will:
- track stock in and out by category and best-before date
- publish a public "most needed items" list for donors that updates itself
- let referral agencies submit e-vouchers that volunteers validate on a tablet at collection
- report on stock levels, items close to expiry, and households supported each month (anonymised)

Constraints: run by volunteers with mixed digital skills; donated hardware (old laptops, Android tablets);
sensitive personal data about households; no budget for paid software licences.

**Good for practising:** open-source licensing decisions, accessibility and UCD for volunteers,
anonymisation and UK GDPR, offline-tolerant design, stock algorithms (FIFO, expiry alerts).
