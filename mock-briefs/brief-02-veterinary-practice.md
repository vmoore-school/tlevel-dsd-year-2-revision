# Mock Brief 02: Pawsitive Vets (short practice brief)

> Original practice scenario. Use it for shorter exercises or a second mock run.

**Pawsitive Vets** is an independent practice with three branches. Clients phone to book
appointments, and repeat prescriptions are requested by email. Staff can't see appointments at other
branches.

The owner wants a solution that will:
- let pet owners book, move and cancel appointments online, choosing a branch and a vet
- let owners request repeat prescriptions, with approval by a vet
- show reception staff a live diary across all branches
- give the owner reports on appointment volumes, no-shows and busiest times

Constraints: pet owner data is personal data; prescription requests must be approved by a qualified
vet; the system must work on reception PCs (Windows) and owners' phones; budget is limited.

**Good for practising:** role-based access (owner / reception / vet / client), date and time handling,
cross-branch data design, approval workflows, KPI dashboards.
