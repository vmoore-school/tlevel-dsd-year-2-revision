## What must be taught

| ID | Status | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 02.03.01 | 🟨 | Risk identification: data & system security (malicious vs accidental), compatibility, speed of development, meeting requirements/KPIs, legal/ethical, user engagement, product reach |
| 02.03.02 | 🟥 | Risk assessment: likelihood x seriousness, risk matrix/scoring |
| 02.03.03 | 🟨 | Impact, mitigation, contingency planning and ongoing monitoring |
| 02.03.04 | ✅ | Policies: backup, security, CIA - revise |
| 02.03.05 | 🟨 | Personnel, skills & training; business continuity planning; disaster recovery planning |
| 02.03.06 | 🟥 | Making and justifying decisions on risk vs reward |

## Suggested activities

- Complete the sample risk register for the mock brief and rank the top 5 risks
- Debate: ship early with known risk vs delay - justify with risk scores
