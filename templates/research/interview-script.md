# Interview Script

**Interviewee role:** client / user / developer · **Technical or non-technical:** · **Date:** · **Consent to record:** Y/N

1. **Warm-up:** What is your role and how do you currently do [task]?
2. **First impressions:** What did you think when you first saw the prototype?
3. **Task-based:** Talk me through how you would [key task]. What was easy or hard?
4. **Requirements check:** Does it do [requirement X] the way you need? Why or why not?
5. **Technical audience only:** How would you rate the code structure, maintainability, security and test coverage?
6. **Priorities:** If we could change one thing next, what should it be?
7. **Close:** Is there anything else you want to tell me?

_Ask open questions and follow up with "Why?" / "Can you show me?". Record answers word for word where you can._
